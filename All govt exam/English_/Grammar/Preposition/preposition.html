📘 Prepositions Guide for SSC, RRB & Competitive Exams

Preposition Use (प्रयोग) Example
atनिश्चित समयI will meet you at 5 PM.
onदिन, तारीखShe was born on Monday.
inमहीना, साल, अवधिHe came in December.
sinceप्रारंभ बिंदु से अब तकShe has been here since 2020.
forअवधि के लिएI stayed there for two days.
beforeकिसी चीज़ से पहलेI will finish it before evening.
afterकिसी चीज़ के बादWe will go after lunch.
byनिश्चित समय तकThe train will arrive by 6 PM.
duringघटना की अवधि मेंIt rained during the night.
from...toशुरू से अंत तकThe office is open from 9 to 5.

PrepositionUse (प्रयोग)Example
inअंदरHe is in the room.
onऊपर (संपर्क में)The book is on the table.
atकिसी बिंदु परShe is at the door.
underनीचेThe cat is under the table.
overऊपर (संपर्क नहीं)The fan is over our heads.
aboveऊपर (स्तर से)The picture is above the bed.
belowनीचे (स्तर से)His marks are below average.
behindपीछेHe is hiding behind the curtain.
in front ofसामनेThe car is in front of the house.
betweenदो के बीचThe ball is between two boxes.
amongतीन या अधिक के बीचHe is among friends.
near / besideपास / बगल मेंShe sat beside me.

✅ Tips to Remember

  • at = बिंदु पर
  • on = सतह पर
  • in = अंदर
  • to = की ओर
  • by = माध्यम से / द्वारा
  • with = किसी वस्तु के प्रयोग से
